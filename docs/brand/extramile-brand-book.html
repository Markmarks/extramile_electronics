01 · Why we're changingAudit

We looked like Apple's settings screen.

The shop runs well. It just doesn't look like anyone in particular. Every colour, radius and font was borrowed from iOS, and the only mark was a letter E in a black square.

Today
  • Colour: iOS systemBlue #007AFF as primary, iOS orange #FF9500 used ad hoc, systemGroupedBackground grey.
  • Mark: a typed "E" in a 28px black square. The desktop app icon is a flat blue square.
  • Type: SF Pro on Apple devices, Inter elsewhere, Segoe UI in the back-office. Three different looks on three machines.
  • Message: none. Nothing on screen says why to buy here over an Instagram seller.
Rebrand
  • Colour: Ink Navy and Sapphire on white by day, Ice Blue on Onyx by night. Platinum, the metal, is kept for one job: paying.
  • Mark: an E whose middle stroke runs past its frame. It's the name drawn as a shape.
  • Type: Bricolage Grotesque for headlines and prices, Inter for reading, JetBrains Mono for IMEIs and order numbers. Bundled, so it looks the same everywhere.
  • Message: the reason to trust us, stated plainly on every page: verified, covered, tracked.
02 · PositioningStrategy

Buying a phone in Accra shouldn't be a gamble.

Most electronics in the city still sell over WhatsApp DMs. Nobody can show you real stock, the warranty is a promise, and a receipt is a screenshot. ExtraMile wins by doing the dull, careful work the DM sellers skip.

Brand promise

Genuine. Guaranteed. Delivered.

Pillar 1

Verified

Every serialised device is IMEI-checked before it's listed, and the number prints on your receipt. The stock count you see is the stock on our shelf.

Pillar 2

Covered

The warranty is a record, not a sentence on a sticker. Months, provider and start date live on your order, and you can look them up any time.

Pillar 3

Tracked

Pay with MoMo or card and you'll know where the order is at every stage, from paid to packed to on its way to East Legon or Kumasi.

04 · LogoMark & wordmark

The stroke that goes past the line.

The mark is an E set in a rounded tile, the shape of a phone or an app. Its middle stroke keeps going after the frame stops. That one stroke is the name: we go the extra mile. It's also the brand's single graphic idea, reused as the order-tracking line and the mile-line rule on this page.

Construction
  1. Tile: 48 × 48 units, 12-unit corner radius, matching the storefront's card radius.
  2. Strokes: all 6.5 units thick. Top and bottom arms are 23 units long.
  3. The extra mile: the middle stroke is 52 units and leaves the tile by 16, a third of the tile's width. It's the only rounded-end stroke and the only one in colour: Stroke Blue, which holds 4:1 contrast on white, Ink Navy and Onyx alike.
  4. Clear space: keep one tile-height ÷ 4 (call it x) empty on every side, measured from the end of the stroke on the right.
  5. Wordmark: "extramile", lowercase, Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold at −5.5% tracking. Lowercase keeps it friendly and stops "ExtraMile" reading as two words.

06 · TypographyThree faces, three jobs

Loud prices, quiet paragraphs, exact numbers.

All three are open-source Google Fonts, loaded through next/font on the web and bundled in the Tauri app, so the counter and the shop finally match.

DisplayAaBricolage Grotesque

Headlines, prices, the wordmark. Its slightly inky, squeezed letters feel made by hand, which keeps big type warm. 680–780 weight.

TextAaInter

Everything you read: specs, buttons, forms, emails. Already in the codebase, so the switch costs nothing. 400–600 weight.

DataAaJetBrains Mono

IMEIs, SKUs, order numbers, MoMo references. Anything a customer might read aloud to support, digit by digit.

10 · RolloutOrder of work

Ship it in five passes.

Each pass is a pull request that leaves both apps working, so nothing waits on a big-bang launch.

Tokens, fonts and night mode

Swap the theme variables, add the Onyx .dark theme that follows the device setting, load the three faces and delete the .glass iOS material. Every screen changes colour in one go.

packages/ui, globals.css, index.css, layout.tsx

Logo component and icons

One <Logo /> SVG component in packages/ui; regenerate the Tauri icon set and the storefront favicon from the square crop.

packages/ui, apps/desktop/src-tauri/icons

Trust UI

Verified and warranty badges on cards and product pages, the platinum MoMo button, and the mile-line order tracker.

apps/web catalog, checkout, account/orders

Documents and messages

Invoice page, Resend emails and SMS copy rewritten in the new voice, with IMEI and warranty end date on every receipt.

invoice page, email templates

Off-screen

Seal stickers for delivery bags, a rider ID card, shop signage and social templates, printed from this book.
